# Credits

## Matt Pocock's skills

Several skills here derive from, or adapt ideas from,
[mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), used under the MIT
license reproduced below. Each has since been rewritten for this toolkit's
backlog, worktree and multi-harness setup, so a difference from the original
is this repo's, not his.

- `grill-me` — from his `grill-me` and `grilling` (round-by-round questioning
  over a frontier of open decisions; its description is his original line).
- `to-tickets` — from his `to-tickets` (tracer-bullet vertical slices with
  blocking edges, expand–contract for wide refactors, prefactoring first).
- `make-skill` — from his `write-a-skill`, `writing-great-skills` and
  `writing-for-agents` (leading words, no-ops, sediment, completion criteria,
  stating the wanted behavior instead of the banned one).
- `backlog-item`'s red/green rules — from his `tdd` and `diagnosing-bugs`
  (test points agreed up front, no tautological tests, proving a forced red).
- `review-diff`'s spec check — from his `code-review` (a spec axis reported
  apart from other findings).
- `retro` — from his `retro` (environment fixes, mechanical problems get a
  check rather than prose).
